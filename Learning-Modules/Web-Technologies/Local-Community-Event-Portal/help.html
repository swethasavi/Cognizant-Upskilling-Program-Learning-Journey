Help & FAQ

How do I register for an event?

Go to the Register section, fill in your name, email, phone and select your event, then click Register Now.

How do I find events near me?

Click Find Nearby Events and allow location access when your browser asks.

Can I save my category preference?

Yes. Selecting a category from the filter saves it automatically. It will be restored on your next visit.

How do I clear saved preferences?

Click Clear Saved Preferences below the registration form.

Community Bulletin

The Coimbatore Community Portal connects residents to local music, sports, art, education and food events held every month across the city.

Our annual Sports Day returns this August at the District Sports Ground. Athletes of all age groups are welcome to register for track and field events.

The Coding Workshop in August is open to complete beginners. No laptop needed as devices will be provided. Seats are limited so register early.

The Food Festival at Market Street is a free entry event with over 40 stalls serving cuisines from across Tamil Nadu. Bring the whole family along.
